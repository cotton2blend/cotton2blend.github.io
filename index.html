About Cotton2Blend

Three decades of excellence in B2B apparel manufacturing

Our Story

Founded in 1995, Cotton2Blend has grown from a small textile manufacturer to a trusted partner for businesses nationwide. We've built our reputation on quality craftsmanship, reliable service, and an unwavering commitment to our clients' success.

Today, we serve hundreds of companies across diverse industries, from tech startups to Fortune 500 corporations, providing them with the premium apparel their brands deserve.

Our Manufacturing Excellence

Sustainable Practices

We believe in responsible manufacturing. Our facilities utilize eco-friendly processes, from water conservation to renewable energy. We source organic and sustainable materials whenever possible, ensuring our products are as good for the planet as they are for your business.

Every step of our production process is designed to minimize environmental impact while maximizing quality and efficiency.
