Finishing Options

Services We Offer

Garment Dye

The fabric is cut and sewn first, then dyed as a finished garment. This creates that soft, lived-in look with slight tonal variation — a premium aesthetic that's highly sought after.

Fabric Dye

The fabric is dyed before construction. This results in a more consistent, uniform color throughout the garment — ideal for clean, bold colorways and precise color matching.

Screen Prints

Individual pieces of fabric are dyed separately before cutting and sewing. This method allows flexibility in mixing tones and is well-suited for larger production runs requiring color consistency.
